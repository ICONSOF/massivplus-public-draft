# MASSIV+ in English

MASSIV+ is developed in Swedish. The specification and the introduction are translated into English for each released version. Where the two languages differ, the Swedish text is authoritative.

- **[Introduction](introduction.md)** - the problem, the idea and the principles. Stands on its own.
- **[Specification](specification.md)** - the full technical description, the normative core of the standard.

Positioning texts, in-depth texts and change proposals are available in Swedish. Comments, issues and proposals are welcome in any language.
